On laptops, the landing page and app screens appear in a centered phone frame. On narrow screens, the frame drops away and the app fills the viewport.

Current build: open `/` to see the URJA landing page, then follow Day Dial → care check → Today. The seeded pocket, schedule, and example movements are **Demo data**. The schedule shows original CSS figure animations for bodyweight squat, wall push-up, and standing march. They are illustrative, not reviewed exercise instruction. In Buddy, enter “aaj 10 minute hi hain” to shorten the schedule and reshape the dial; Undo restores the prior state. Studio provides an animated guided timer only. It has no camera, pose model, rep counter, or form cues. Meals are not implemented because verified food data is absent. The `/kit` route remains available for the visual foundation.
